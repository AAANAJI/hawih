# Product Prompts

One filled prompt per demo product. Palette locked to the style guide (cobalt `#1F1FFE`
sole accent, paper `#F4F1EB`/`#E9E4D7` grounds, ink `#0B0B10` shadows). Append the shared
negatives block to every generation. weej source images are **composition reference only** —
mimic framing, never copy artwork or branding.

Shared suffix (append to each prompt):
> Studio product photography, soft top-left key light, soft contact shadow on a warm
> paper seamless (#F4F1EB to #E9E4D7). Abstract cobalt (#1F1FFE) geometric artwork on any
> printed surface. High-end, editorial, generous negative space. webp master.
> Negatives: no watermarks, no competitor brands or logos, no real phone numbers or QR
> codes, no people or hands, no busy background, no multi-color palette, no harsh
> reflections, no text-heavy surfaces, no stock-photo look, no low-res artifacts.
