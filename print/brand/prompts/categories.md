# Category Tile Prompts

One filled prompt per category. Category tiles are **1200×900 (4:3)** with more negative
space than product shots — one representative product, room for an overlaid label. Palette
locked to the style guide. Append the shared negatives block. weej source images are
**composition reference only**.

Shared suffix (append to each prompt):
> Studio product photography, soft top-left key light, soft contact shadow on a warm paper
> seamless (#F4F1EB to #E9E4D7). Sole accent cobalt #1F1FFE as abstract geometry on printed
> surfaces. Generous negative space for a text label. High-end editorial. webp master.
> Negatives: no watermarks, no competitor brands or logos, no phone numbers or QR codes,
> no people, no busy background, no multi-color palette, no harsh reflections, no
> text-heavy surfaces, no stock-photo look, no low-res artifacts.
