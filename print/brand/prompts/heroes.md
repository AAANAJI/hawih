# Hero & Social Prompts

Home hero banner and OG/social card. Palette locked to the style guide (cobalt `#1F1FFE`
sole accent, paper `#F4F1EB`/`#E9E4D7` grounds, ink `#0B0B10` shadows). Append the shared
negatives block. weej source images are **composition reference only**.

Shared suffix (append to each prompt):
> Studio product photography / soft photoreal render, soft top-left key light, contact
> shadows on a warm paper seamless (#F4F1EB to #E9E4D7). Sole accent cobalt #1F1FFE as
> abstract geometry. High-end, editorial, calm. webp master.
> Negatives: no watermarks, no competitor brands or logos, no phone numbers or QR codes,
> no people or hands, no busy background, no multi-color palette, no harsh reflections, no
> text-heavy surfaces, no stock-photo look, no low-res artifacts.

---

## home-hero (2400×1200, 2:1 banner)
A wide editorial arrangement of premium print products drifting across a warm paper
seamless — a stack of matte business cards, a kraft box, a handled paper bag, a fan of
round stickers, an invitation in its envelope — all unified by abstract cobalt (#1F1FFE)
geometric artwork (lines, arcs, concentric rings) printed on the samples. Composition
weighted to the **right two-thirds**, leaving the **left third open** for an Arabic
headline set in IBM Plex Sans Arabic. Soft depth of field, cohesive cobalt-on-paper look.
_Composition ref: weej homepage hero — layout only, never copy products or branding._

## og-social (1200×630, 1.91:1)
A brand-forward centered still: a small curated trio — matte business cards, a round
sticker, and a kraft box — grouped center-frame on the paper seamless with a single bold
cobalt geometric motif behind them. Balanced, safe centered composition (no text baked in;
overlay handled at render). Premium, minimal, instantly on-brand.
_Composition ref: generic print-shop OG cards — layout only._
